Open on your phone

AR runs on an iPhone, or an Android phone with ARCore. Scan the code with the camera.

How it works

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap View in AR.
  2. Point the camera at a table and move the phone slowly.
  3. Drag the model to move it, pinch to resize it.
