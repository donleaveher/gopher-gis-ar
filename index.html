Open on your phone

AR needs Safari on an iPhone, or Chrome on an Android phone with ARCore. Scan the code with the phone's camera to open this page there.

How it works

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap View in AR.
  2. Point the camera at a table and move the phone slowly.
  3. Drag the model to move it, pinch to resize it.
